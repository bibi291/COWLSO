THE THEME

Breaking Boundaries

"Breaking Boundaries" speaks to the courage it takes for women to step past the limits placed on them — by tradition, by circumstance, or by self-doubt — and to build community on the other side of that line. The 2025 conference brought together women from different backgrounds, professions and generations to challenge those boundaries together, through honest conversation, shared skills and collective support.
