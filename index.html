WHO WE ARE

About COWLSO

COWLSO is a Nigerian women's organisation dedicated to bringing women together, strengthening communities and creating opportunities for positive social impact.

Through conferences, community initiatives and specialised divisions, COWLSO provides a platform where women can connect, share knowledge, support one another and contribute to the development of their communities.

Our work is built around collaboration, service, sustainability and the belief that when women come together, meaningful change can happen.
