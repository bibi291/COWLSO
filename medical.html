WHO WE ARE

Medical Division

The Medical Division is COWLSO's hands-on response to healthcare gaps in the communities we serve. Through outreach, screenings, education and direct support, we bring care to people who might otherwise go without it.

It isn't about grand gestures — it's about showing up, consistently, with the women and families who need us. Every outreach is planned, staffed and run by COWLSO members who believe healthcare is a community effort.
